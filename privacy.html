About This Policy

High Yield is an MCAT-themed charades and study game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our iPhone app.

Information Saved on Your Device

High Yield stores game information locally, including scores, study progress, missed words, response times, settings, favorites, custom decks, card-cycle history, team and player names you enter, and interrupted-round recovery data.

This information supports gameplay, personalized review, and resuming unfinished rounds. The current app does not require an account or upload these local game records to a developer-operated server. We do not intentionally send your entered team names, custom decks, or study records to Google for advertising.

Advertising and Google AdMob

High Yield integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google's advertising SDK may collect device or app identifiers, IP address and approximate location, advertising interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information.

Google may use this information to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, and maintain or improve its services. Processing depends on your permissions, privacy choices, location, and the app's configuration. Test advertisements may also involve SDK data processing.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google's iOS advertising data disclosures.

Privacy Choices

Where applicable, the app presents privacy messages through Google's consent-management service. When available, you can reopen Ad privacy choices under App status on the home screen.

You can also manage relevant permissions through your device settings. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertising-related data processing, such as fraud prevention or measurement.

Motion Controls

High Yield uses device motion and orientation signals for tilt-based gameplay. The app processes these signals on your device and does not intentionally upload or retain raw motion recordings. Tap controls are also available.

Support and Sharing

If you contact us, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address and message, and use it to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send sensitive personal or medical information.

When you choose to share a result, the selected sharing service receives the content you send. Its own privacy policy applies.

Retention and Deletion

Local game information remains on your device until removed through available reset controls, deletion of the app and its data, or device storage management. Individual reset controls may remove only a particular category of information. Device backups may retain copies.

We cannot directly access or remotely delete records stored only on your device. Deleting the app does not automatically delete information previously processed by Google or another service. Their retention and deletion policies apply.

Support correspondence is retained as reasonably needed to address your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising-related uses or disclosures.

Contact us using the email above to submit a request. We may need information to verify your request. For information processed independently by Google, consult Google's privacy controls and policies.

Children

High Yield is intended for college-age and adult learners, not children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Security and International Processing

We use reasonable safeguards, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Third-party providers may process information outside your state or country, as described in their privacy policies.
